Add this ONE line to Program.cs, right before `app.UseStaticFiles();`:

    Directory.CreateDirectory(Path.Combine(app.Environment.ContentRootPath, "wwwroot"));
    app.UseStaticFiles();

That's the entire fix for the "WebRootPath was not found" warning - ASP.NET Core
warns because wwwroot doesn't exist yet at startup (LocalFileStorageService only
creates its uploads subfolder lazily, on first request). This makes sure wwwroot
itself exists before UseStaticFiles() tries to serve from it.
